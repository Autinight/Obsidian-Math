### 17:04


若
$$
\left\| u \right\|_{q}\le C\left\| Du \right\|_{p} 
$$
则
$$
\left\| u_{q} \right\|= \left(\int \left| u \right|^{q} \right) ^{\frac{n}{q}}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned} \left\| u_{\lambda } \right\|_{q}&= \left(\int \left| u\left(\lambda x \right) \right|^{q} \,d x\right) ^{\frac{q}{1}}\\&= \left(\int \left| u\left(x\right) \right| ^{q}\frac{1 }{\lambda^{n}  }\,d x\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}\\\\&= \lambda ^{-\frac{n}{q}} \left\| u \right\|_{q} \end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned} \left\| Du \right\|_{p}&=  \left(\int \left| Du\left(\lambda x\right) \right|^{p} \right) ^{\frac{1}{p}}\\&= \left(\int \left| \lambda \left(Du\right)_{\lambda x} \right| ^{p}\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\,d x\\&= \lambda \left(\frac{1}{\lambda }\int \left| Du \right|^{p} \,d x\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\\&= \lambda ^{1-\frac{n}{p}}\left\| Du \right\|_{p}\end{aligned} 
$$
于是

$$
1-\frac{n }{p }= -\frac{n }{q },\quad \frac{n }{q }= \frac{n }{p }-1,\quad \frac{1 }{q }= \frac{1 }{p }-\frac{1 }{n },\quad \frac{1 }{q }= \frac{n-p }{np }\implies q= \frac{np }{n-p } 
$$
