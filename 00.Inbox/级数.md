设
$$
\frac{a_{n} }{ a_{n+ 1}}= 1+ \frac{r }{n } + o\left(\frac{1 }{n^{\varepsilon } }\right) 
$$

$$
\frac{\frac{1 }{n } }{\frac{1 }{n+ 1 } }= 1+ \frac{1 }{n } 
$$

$$
\frac{1 }{n \left(\ln n\right)^{p} } 
$$

$$
a_{n}= \frac{1 }{n\left(\ln n\right)^{p} } 
$$

$$
\frac{a_{n} }{a_{n+ 1} }= \frac{n+ 1\left(\ln \left(n+ 1\right)\right)^{p} }{n\left(\ln n\right)^{p} }= \left(1+ \frac{1 }{n }\right)\left(\ln \left(1+ \frac{1 }{n }\right)\right)^{p} 
$$




$$
\int _{2}^{\infty}\frac{1 }{x }\frac{1 }{\left(\ln x\right)^{p} }\,d x=  \int _{\ln 2}^{\infty}\frac{1 }{t^{p} }\,d t
$$
当$p\le 1$时发散, 当$p> 1$时收敛.

$$
\int _{n}^{n+ 1}f\left(x\right)\,d x\le f\left(n\right) \le \int _{n-1}^{n}f\left(x\right)\,d x
$$
$$
\int _{N}^{m+ 1}f\left(x\right)\,d x\le \sum _{n = N}^{m}f\left(n\right) \le f\left(N\right)+ \sum _{N}^{m}f\left(x\right)\,d x
$$

令
$$
b_{n}= \frac{1 }{n \left(\ln n\right)^{r}} 
$$

$$
\frac{b_{n} }{b_{n+ 1} }= \left(1+ \frac{1 }{n }\right) \frac{\left(\ln \left(n+ 1\right)\right)^{r} }{\left(\ln n\right)^{r} }
$$

$$
\ln \left(n+ 1\right) -\ln n =  \ln \left(1+ \frac{1 }{n }\right)
= 1+ \frac{1 }{n }+ O\left(\frac{1 }{n^{2} }\right)$$

于是
$$
\ln \left(n+ 1\right)=\ln n+\frac{1 }{n }+  O\left(\frac{1 }{n^{2} }\right) 
$$

$$
\frac{\ln \left(n+ 1\right) }{\ln n }= 1+ \frac{1 }{n\ln n }+ O\left(\frac{1 }{n^{2}\ln n }\right) 
$$

$$
\left(\frac{\ln \left(n+ 1\right) }{\ln n }\right)^{r}= 1+ \frac{r }{n\ln n }+ O\left(\frac{1 }{n^{2}\ln n }\right) 
$$

$$
\begin{aligned} \begin{aligned} \frac{b_{n} }{b_{n+ 1} }&= \frac{n+ 1 }{ n}\left(1+ \frac{r }{n\ln n }\right)+ O\left(\frac{1 }{n^{2}\ln n }\right)\\&= 1+ \frac{1 }{n }+ \frac{n+ 1 }{n^{2} }\frac{r }{\ln n }+ O\left(\frac{1 }{n^{2}\ln n }\right)\\&= 1+ \frac{1 }{n }+ \frac{p }{n\ln n }+ O\left(\frac{1 }{n^{2}\ln n }\right) \end{aligned}\end{aligned}
$$

若
$$
\frac{a_{n} }{a_{n+ 1} }= 1+ \frac{1 }{n }+ \frac{r }{n\ln n }+ O\left(\frac{1 }{n^{2}\ln n }\right) 
$$

若$r> 1$, 则存在 $p$使得$r> p> 1$, 我们有级数
$$
\sum b_{n}= \sum  \frac{1 }{n\left(\ln n\right)^{p} } 
$$
收敛, 此时
$$
\frac{a_{n} }{a_{n+ 1} }-\frac{b_{n} }{b_{n+ 1} }= \left(r-p\right)\frac{1 }{n\ln n }+ O\left(\frac{1 }{n^{2}\ln n }\right) 
$$
存在$N$, 使得
$$
\frac{a_{n} }{a_{n+ 1} }\ge \frac{b_{n} }{b_{n+ 1} } 
$$
那么
$$
\frac{a_{N} }{a_{n+ 1} }\ge \frac{b_{N} }{b_{n+ 1} } 
$$

$$
a_{n+ 1}\le b_{n+ 1}\frac{a_{N} }{ b_{N}} 
$$
于是$\left\{ a_{n} \right\}$收敛.



## Important Intuition

> [!note] Note: 
> 对数步长约为$\frac{1 }{n }$
>
> $$
> \ln \left(n+ 1\right)-\ln n\sim \frac{1 }{n }
>$$
>所以$n$大约是对数步长的倒数
>$$
>n\sim \frac{1 }{\ln \left(n+ 1\right)-\ln n } 
> $$
