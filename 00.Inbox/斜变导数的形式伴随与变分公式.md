
$$ \nabla ^{*}=- \operatorname{tr}_{g}\left(\nabla \right) $$ 

设$T$是速度场, $V$是变分场, 则

曲线$c:I\to M$的切向量场

令$E$是$I$上的拉回丛$\bar{c}^{*}TM$, 则

$$ \nabla :\Gamma \left(E\right)\to \Gamma \left(T^{*}I\otimes E\right) $$ 
$$ \nabla V = \left(\nabla _{\frac{\partial }{\partial t}}V\right)\otimes dt $$ 

$$ \nabla ^{*}:T\left(T ^{*}I\otimes E\right)\to \Gamma \left(E\right) $$ 

$$ \nabla ^{*}\left(T\otimes \,d t\right)= \nabla _{\frac{\partial }{\partial t}}\left(T\otimes \,d t\right)\left(\frac{\partial }{\partial t}\right)= -\nabla _{\frac{\partial }{\partial t}}T  $$ 

于是变分公式
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{d E\left(c_{s}\right)}{d s} &= -\int_{a}^{b} g\left(\frac{\partial^{2} \bar{c}}{\partial t^{2}}, \frac{\partial \bar{c}}{\partial s}\right) d t+\left.g\left(\frac{\partial \bar{c}}{\partial t^{-}}, \frac{\partial \bar{c}}{\partial s}\right)\right|_{(s, b)}-\left.g\left(\frac{\partial \bar{c}}{\partial t^{+}}, \frac{\partial \bar{c}}{\partial s}\right)\right|_{(s, a)} \\
&\quad +\left.\sum_{i=1}^{m-1} g\left(\frac{\partial \bar{c}}{\partial t^{-}}-\frac{\partial \bar{c}}{\partial t^{+}}, \frac{\partial \bar{c}}{\partial s}\right)\right|_{\left(s, a_{i}\right)} .
\end{aligned}
$$

写成是
$$ \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}s}E\left(c_{s}\right)= -\int _{a}^{b}g\left(\nabla ^{*}\left(T\otimes dt\right), V \right) + \text{边界项}$$
