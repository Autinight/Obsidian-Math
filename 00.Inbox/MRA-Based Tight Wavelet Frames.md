
> [!definition] Definition: Dyadic Dilation Operator and Translation Operator
> For any function $f\in L^{2}\left(\mathbb{R} \right)$, 
> - the **dyadic dilation operator** $\mathcal{D}$ is defined by
>     $$ \mathcal{D}f\left(x\right):= \sqrt{2}f\left(2x\right) $$ 
> - and the **translation operator** $T$ is defined 
>     $$  T_{t}f\left(x\right):= f\left(x-t\right)$$ 

> [!definition] Definition: Wavelet System(Affine System)
> For give $\Psi := \left\{ \psi _1,\cdots ,\psi _{r} \right\}\subseteq L^{2}\left(\mathbb{R} \right)$, define the **wavelet system** as 
> $$ X\left(\Psi \right):= \left\{ \psi _{\ell ,n,k}: 1\le \ell \le r, n,k \in \mathbb{Z}  \right\} $$
> where 
> $$ \psi _{\ell ,n,k}= D^{n}T_{k}\psi _{\ell }= 2^{\frac{n}{2}}\psi _{\ell }\left(2^{n}x-k\right) $$
