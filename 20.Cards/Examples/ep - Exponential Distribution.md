## Memoryless Property

> [!proposition] Proposition: 
>  $$ P\left(X> s+ t|X> t\right)= P\left(X> s\right) $$ 

> [!proposition] Proposition: 
> $\min \left\{ X,Y \right\}$与事件$\chi _{\left\{ X\ge Y \right\}}$独立

> [!note] Note: 
> 比赛结束的时间不会包含赢家的信息.
