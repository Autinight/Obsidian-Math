> [!definition] General Linear Lie Algebra $\mathfrak{gl}(n,\mathbb{R})$
> The notation $\mathfrak{gl}(n,\mathbb{R})$ denotes the **Lie algebra of the general linear group**.
> 
> 1.  **As a Vector Space**: It consists of the set of **all** $n \times n$ matrices with real entries (often denoted as $M_n(\mathbb{R})$). 
> 2.  **Lie Bracket**: It is equipped with the standard matrix commutator as the Lie bracket:
>     $$\begin{aligned}[A, B] = AB - BA\end{aligned}$$
>     
>     for any $A, B \in \mathfrak{gl}(n,\mathbb{R})$.
