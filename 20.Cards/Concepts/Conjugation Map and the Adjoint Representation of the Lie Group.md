> [!definition] Definition: 
> Let $G$ be a Lie group and $\mathfrak{g}$ be its Lie algebra. 
> * For any $g \in G$, the conjugation map $C_g: G \to G$ given by $C_g(h) = ghg^{-1}$ is a Lie group homomorphism . 
> * We let $\operatorname{Ad}(g) = (C_g)_*: \mathfrak{g} \to \mathfrak{g}$ denote its induced Lie algebra homomorphism.

> [!proposition] Proposition (The Adjoint Representation)
> If $G$ is a Lie group with Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g}$, the map $\operatorname{Ad}:G\to\operatorname{GL}(\mathfrak{g})$ is a Lie group representation, called the **adjoint representation of $G$**.
