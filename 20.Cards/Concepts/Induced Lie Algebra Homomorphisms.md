## Uniqueness of Related Left-invariant Vector fields

> [!theorem] Induced Lie Algebra Homomorphisms: 
> Let $G$ and $H$ be Lie groups, and let $\mathfrak{g}$ and $\mathfrak{h}$ be their Lie algebras. Suppose $F: G \to H$ is a Lie group homomorphism. For every $X \in \mathfrak{g}$, there is a unique vector field in $\mathfrak{h}$ that is $F$-related to $X$. With this vector field denoted by $F_*X$, the map $F_*: \mathfrak{g} \to \mathfrak{h}$ so defined is a Lie algebra homomorphism.

## Deifinition

> [!definition] Definition: 
> The map $F_*: \mathfrak{g} \to \mathfrak{h}$ whose existence is asserted in this theorem is called the **induced Lie algebra homomorphism**. 

## Functorility 

> [!proposition] Properties of Induced Homomorphisms
> * (a) The homomorphism $(\operatorname{Id}_G)_*: \operatorname{Lie}(G) \to \operatorname{Lie}(G)$ induced by the identity map of $G$ is the identity of $\operatorname{Lie}(G)$.
> * (b) If $F_1: G \to H$ and $F_2: H \to K$ are Lie group homomorphisms, then
> $$(F_2 \circ F_1)_* = (F_2)_* \circ (F_1)_*: \operatorname{Lie}(G) \to \operatorname{Lie}(K).$$
> * (c) Isomorphic Lie groups have isomorphic Lie algebras.
