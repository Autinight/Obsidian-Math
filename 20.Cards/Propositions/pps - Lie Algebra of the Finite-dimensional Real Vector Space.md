> [!corollary] Corollary
> If $V$ is any finite-dimensional real vector space, the composition of the canonical isomorphisms in [[pps - Lie Algebra of the General Linear Group|Lie Algebra of the General Linear Group]] yields a Lie algebra isomorphism between $\operatorname{Lie}(\operatorname{GL}(V))$ and $\mathfrak{gl}(V)$.
