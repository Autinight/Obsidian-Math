> [!proposition] Naturality of Integral Curves
> Suppose $M$ and $N$ are smooth manifolds and $F:M\to N$ is a smooth map. Then $X\in\mathfrak{X}(M)$ and $Y\in\mathfrak{X}(N)$ are $F$-related if and only if $F$ takes integral curves of $X$ to integral curves of $Y$, meaning that for each integral curve $\gamma$ of $X$, $F\circ\gamma$ is an integral curve of $Y$.
