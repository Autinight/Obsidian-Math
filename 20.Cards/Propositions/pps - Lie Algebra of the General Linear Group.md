> [!proposition] Lie Algebra of the General Linear Group
> The composition of the natural maps
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> \operatorname{Lie}(\operatorname{GL}(n,\mathbb{R})) \to T_{I_n} \operatorname{GL}(n,\mathbb{R}) \to \mathfrak{gl}(n,\mathbb{R})
> \end{aligned}
> $$
> gives a Lie algebra isomorphism between $\operatorname{Lie}(\operatorname{GL}(n,\mathbb{R}))$ and the matrix algebra $\mathfrak{gl}(n,\mathbb{R})$.
