## Preliminaries

> [!proposition] Proposition:
> Let $G$ be a Lie group, and suppose $X$ and $Y$ are smooth left-invariant vector fields on $G$. Then $[X,Y]$ is also left-invariant.

## Definition

> [!definition] Definition: 
> The Lie algebra of all smooth left-invariant vector fields on a Lie group $G$ is called the **Lie algebra of $G$**, and is denoted by $\operatorname{Lie}(G)$.
