> [!definition] Definition: 
> Given a finite-dimensional Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g}$, for each $X\in\mathfrak{g}$, define a map $\operatorname{ad}(X): \mathfrak{g}\to\mathfrak{g}$ by $\operatorname{ad}(X)Y=[X, Y]$.

> [!proposition] Proposition:
> For any Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g}$, the map $\operatorname{ad}: \mathfrak{g}\to\mathfrak{gl}(\mathfrak{g})$ is a Lie algebra representation, called the **adjoint representation of $\mathfrak{g}$**.
