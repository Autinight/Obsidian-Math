> [!theorem] The Lie Algebra of a Lie Subgroup
> Suppose $H \subseteq G$ is a Lie subgroup, and $\iota: H \hookrightarrow G$ is the inclusion map. There is a Lie subalgebra $\mathfrak{h} \subseteq \operatorname{Lie}(G)$ that is canonically isomorphic to $\operatorname{Lie}(H)$, characterized by either of the following descriptions:
> $$\begin{aligned} \mathfrak{h} &= \iota_*(\operatorname{Lie}(H)) \\ &= \{X \in \operatorname{Lie}(G) : X_e \in T_e H\}. \end{aligned}$$
