> [!theorem] First Isomorphism Theorem for Lie Groups
> If $F:G\to H$ is a Lie group homomorphism, then the kernel of $F$ is a closed normal Lie subgroup of $G$, the image of $F$ has a unique smooth manifold structure making it into a Lie subgroup of $H$, and $F$ descends to a Lie group isomorphism $\widetilde{F}:G/\operatorname{Ker}F\to\operatorname{Im}F$. If $F$ is surjective, then $G/\operatorname{Ker}F$ is smoothly isomorphic to $H$.
