> [!theorem] Theorem: 
>  Let $G$ be a Lie group. The evaluation map $\varepsilon: \operatorname{Lie}(G) \rightarrow T_e G$, given by $\varepsilon(X) = X_e$, is a vector space isomorphism. Thus, $\operatorname{Lie}(G)$ is finite-dimensional, with dimension equal to $\dim G$.

> [!remark] Remark: 
> A left-invariant (rough) vector field is determined by its value at $e$.
