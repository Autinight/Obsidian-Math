> [!theorem] Theorem
> Let $G$ be a Lie group, let $\mathfrak{g}$ be its Lie algebra, and let $\operatorname{Ad}: G \to \operatorname{GL}(\mathfrak{g})$ be the adjoint representation of $G$. The induced Lie algebra representation $\operatorname{Ad}_*: \mathfrak{g} \to \operatorname{gl}(\mathfrak{g})$ is given by $\operatorname{Ad}_* = \operatorname{ad}$.
