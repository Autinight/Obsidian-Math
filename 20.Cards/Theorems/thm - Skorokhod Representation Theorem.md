> [!theorem] Theorem: 
> If $S$ is a seperable metric space, $X_{n}$ ,$X$ are random variables value in $S$. If 
>   $$ X_{n}\xrightarrow{d} X  $$
> Then there exists a probability space $\left(\Omega ,\mathcal{F},P\right)$ and random variables $Y_{n}$ , $Y$ defined there, such that 
> 1.   $$ Y_{n}\xrightarrow{d}X_{n},\quad Y\xrightarrow{d}X   $$
> 2.  $$ Y_{n}\xrightarrow{a.s.}Y  $$
