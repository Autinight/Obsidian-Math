### 10:14


设$A\in \mathscr{L}\left(H\right)$,

共轭算子
$$ A^{*} $$ 

$$ \left(Ax,y\right)= \left(x, A^{*}y\right) $$ 

对于任意的$x$, 定义
$$ \left(A^{*}y,x\right)= \left(Ax,y\right) $$ 

如果
$$ \left(f_1,x\right)= \left(f_2,x\right),\forall x $$ 

则
$$ \left(f_1-f_2,x\right)= 0 $$ 

$$ f_1-f_2= 0 $$ 


$$ \left(A+ B\right)^{*}= A^{*}+ B^{*} $$ 

$$ \left(\alpha Ax,y\right)= \left(x, \left(\alpha A\right)^{*}\right) $$ 

$$ \left(\alpha Ax,y\right)= \alpha \left(Ax,y\right)= \alpha \left(x,A^{*}y\right)= \left(x,\overline{\alpha}A^{*}y\right)= pa $$ 

$$ \left(\alpha A\right)^{*}= \overline{\alpha}A^{*} $$ 


$$ \left(Ax,y\right)= \left(x, A^{*}y\right)= \left(A^{*}y,x\right)= \left(y, A^{**}x\right) $$ 

$$ \left(ABx,y\right)= \left(Bx,A^{*}y\right)= \left(x,B^{*}A^{*}y\right) $$
